# Kannon Arena family playtest

Kannon Arena is now a first-person game: you see your hands and your weapon. Refresh [Kannon Arena](https://kpop.8westventures.com) on both devices first. The play screen should show **Your loadout** with a class such as Assault. Turn the iPhone sideways. Safari's bars take about a third of the screen then, so tap **Play full screen** on the ready screen to see how to hide them. Keep each player's saved key private.

1. Choose **Create match**, pick **Free-for-all** and **Private**, and share the invitation. Or choose **Public** and have the other player use **Available Games → Join**. Turn **Fill to 4 players with bots** on for AI rivals or off for people only. The host starts, then each player selects **Ready to play** once the arena appears.
2. In the lobby, tap **Change** under **Your loadout** and pick a class: Assault, Close Quarters, Marksman or Custom. You can change it again while waiting to respawn; the new class starts with your next life.
3. On the phone, hold the left stick and look around with a second finger on the right. Lift only the left thumb: you should stop moving while looking still works. Push the stick all the way forward to sprint, and push again quickly to dash. Repeat after rotating the phone, pausing and switching away from Safari. Coming back needs a fresh tap.
4. Start with **Settings → Mobile firing → Simple**. Rest the crosshair on a rival. Your weapon should fire without pressing Fire, and stop when the crosshair moves off. Walls and spawn protection stop it.
5. Try **Advanced**: hold Fire and drag the same finger to aim. Release to stop. Try both Fire buttons, **Aim**, **Crouch** (press it while sprinting to slide), **Jump** into a low wall to climb over it, **Reload**, **Swap**, **Grenade** (hold to cook, let go to throw) and **Knife**.
6. Take a little damage, then hide. Your health should start to refill after about 4 seconds.
7. Get eliminations in a row: 3 earns **Recon Sweep**, 5 earns **Mortar Volley** (tap it, then pick a spot on the map) and 7 earns **Attack Drone**. Tap the reward button to use it.
8. Play one **Team Deathmatch** match. Teammates show in blue and enemies in red. Check that your teammates cannot hurt you and that the team score at the top goes up.
9. Optionally select **Enable gyroscope aiming**, allow the motion request and tilt gently. Adjust its own sensitivity. Turning it off goes back to touch-only aiming; declining permission leaves touch usable.
10. Explore both houses, the stairs inside and out, the upstairs windows, the yards, the open truck and the new sandbag walls and barriers. Play a full round, compare results and crew standings, then rematch. Report stuck doorways, unfair spawns, sightlines that feel too strong, or shots that looked like hits but did not count.

Tell Mary the iPhone model, the browser, Wi-Fi or mobile data, and your preferred firing mode. The most useful first results: whether lifting the movement thumb stops you on **both** screens, whether the game feels smooth on the iPhone, and whether shots at moving players count. Also report how well you can see other players and how the gyroscope feels. A short description is enough.

Also try **Play against bots → difficulty → Create bot match → Enter bot match → Ready to play** when playing alone. For a public casual match with bots, check that one person can start and that a second person takes a bot's place when joining the lobby before the host starts. Private rooms should never appear in Available Games.

This retest gives the real-phone and family feedback that browser tests cannot.
